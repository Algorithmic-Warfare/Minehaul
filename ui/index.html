Algorithmic Warfare · v0 alpha

Minehaul UI

Chamfered plates and countable stacks for logistics on EVE Frontier. Plain CSS and small ES modules, with no build step. Seven palette swatches drive every colour, and the AWAR palette is the default.

Palette

Swap the swatches, keep the system

Every role, state and chart colour is derived with var() and color-mix() from these seven tokens. Change them here or override them in your own :root. The chamfer depth is a token too: set --mh-cut-scale: 0 for AWAR's square corners.

      Stacks
      
The open board, as countable cargo

      
Columns are reward-rate bands. Each square is 100 units, and each job is one unbroken run. Drag the floor to see which jobs clear your rate. Hatched jobs carry a risk, such as an expiring listing or a leased SSU that leaves before the deadline. Click any square, row or bar to open the job below.

    

    

  


  
  

    

      Job
      
One Transfer, end to end

      
Rendered by renderJobCard() from a record shaped like HaulAction. It shows the endpoints (no travel path), the reward and the collateral minimum (rounded up), the lifecycle, the timing that applies to the current state, the fixed payout split, and the manifest.

    

    

  


  
  

    

      Stacks, regrouped
      
Escrow by stage

      
This is the same component grouped by lifecycle stage instead of rate. A square is 2,000 CR held in escrow: the reward, plus collateral once a job is claimed. Your hauls take the brand colour through a single --mh-data-match override.

    

    

      

      

    

  


  
  

    

      Track
      
Every lifecycle state

      
Interlocking plates follow ActionStatus. Delivering is skipped because the delivery handoff commits atomically. Disputes, resolutions, expiries and cancellations leave as a hatched exit, so they read without colour.
